# NazRiy Administrator Guide

Sign in at `/admin/`. The fixed sidebar groups work into Sales, Catalogue, Storefront, and Customer Access.

## Catalogue

- **Products:** add photographs, names, descriptions, prices, sizes, colours, and stock. Disable **Active** to hide a product without deleting order history. **Featured** controls featured feeds.
- **Categories:** edit the clothing category, image, alt text, display order, and featured state.
- **Homepage products:** choose products, optional showcase images, activation, and ordering.

## Storefront

- **Hero banners:** manage desktop/mobile images, alt text, buttons, schedule, theme, and order.
- **Navigation menu:** activate, deactivate, reorder, and change destination paths without editing code.

## Sales and fulfilment

- **Orders:** inspect customer details and immutable line items. Move confirmed orders to shipped or cancelled; shipped orders may move to delivered. Cancellation restores inventory once.
- **Payments:** filter by method/status. For bKash, compare order amount and submitted transaction ID with the merchant statement, then run **Verify selected bKash payments** or **Reject selected bKash payments**. Never request or store a PIN or OTP.
- COD payments can be updated through the individual payment record according to the operational process.

## Routine operations

1. Review low-stock cards and order queue daily.
2. Back up before bulk changes or releases.
3. Use least-privilege staff groups; do not share a superuser account.
4. Review application logs without copying secrets or personal data into tickets.
